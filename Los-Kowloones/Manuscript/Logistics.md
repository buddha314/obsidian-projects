Six Years Earlier (From the Seven Years Later)

Boxes of mixed sizes crowded the rented warehouse.  A small team in jeans, not robes in the real world, were printing packing slips, moving them towards the door.  Terence, who was used to lifting up to 50 pounds as part of his employment, began loading them into his brown truck.  Different colors, different weights, different brands. This one was a friendly yellow color. It was off to save the broiling hellscape that was Los Angeles.
