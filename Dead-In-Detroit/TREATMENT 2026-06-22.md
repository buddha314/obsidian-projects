## ACT TWO
Jack is crushed, has lost his job and is relying on his bro network to follow events. He sets out across America to find "real metal" and wanders a zombie infested country risking his life to see local bands. He starts a social media campaign with very few followers. He gets one heart from ZERO

word begins to get out that metal might save the world, but nobody is listening.

Interstitial LARS is making enormous amounts of cash selling shitty, knock off bands to WAD who has no taste.  Montage of him reacting to various crappy bands and trying to be hip.

JACK finds Dolores' home, trying to identify the magic playlist. She and her phone have been eaten.
## MIDPOINT

Zombies break out of the containment center in Detroit. This is JACK's fault. After the failed experiment the whole thing is getting defunded and the gates are basically left open. A few bands head that way, hoping to save the world. JACK doesn't know this.

JACK spends the bottom of ACT II trying to get everyone to run and hide, he has given up on music.

Indie bands race across the US. Their only weapon is the might of their rock. JACK is depressed that he has been listening to the same shit for decades, forgetting the real metal comes from a shitty amp in a garage.
## BREAK THREE

Jack logs back into social media and see hundreds of bands heading to Detroit. They believe in the power of metal.

## ACT THREE

BAND has a loose affiliation with a Narco Corridos band that holds off some zombies. They are feeling like the heroes right now.

JACK figures out a way to get the Army to do _one last try_ and broadcast a live concert to see what works. The winner gets to save the world live on the internet.

Getting close to the radio station, it turns into Rollerball / The Warriors, the bands are trying to wipe each other out. At first BAND goes unnoticed but the other bands start hearing them and variously think "oh fuck, we have to kill them" or "oh fuck, they are going to save the world, we need to wing man for them." The true metalheads take the latter route.

The Army has lost control of basically all of Michigan, so they have to fight each other and zombies to even make it to the station. LARS of course is there ready to sign the deal with the winner.

During the climax, our Narco Corridos desperately grab a dead bands gear and play some metaled-out ballads, a few zombies die before they are eaten.

Anyway, our heroes have to go up against, I guess NELSON. During the NELSON set the zombies have taken the arena and everyone is dying. They are about to abandon the stage when ZERO's void breaks through the crowd and all the good feels happen.

After that, LARS gets rebuked and eaten.

JACK waives his fees.

BAND is the most famous band in the world.
