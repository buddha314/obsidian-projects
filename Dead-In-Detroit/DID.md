INt. underground zombie laboratory - night

DOLORES, the cleaning lady, pushes a mop through sinister underground hallways. She is wearing a standard cleaning outfit. A sign reads UNITED STATES DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY: ZOMBIE CONTROL.

CCTV POV (SILENT)

DOLORES Looks directly into the camera, holds up her phone so it is clearly visible. She is taunting someone. She presses "PLAY" on a playlist title "Narco Corridos".

CLOSE UP

On the cart a bluetooth speaker comes on.

BACK TO SCENE

A Narcocorrido ballad is suddenly blasting Mexican clipping out speakers. DOLORES line steps and sings in Spanish as she moves along. A string pulls a WET FLOOR sign behind a cart. Several RESEARCHERS in full Quarantine PPE, they pause to watch her pass, shaking their heads.

She mops and dances past windows that reveal ZOMBIES in various stages of agitation. Some notice her, most ignore. She suddenly stops and looks ahead, a sense of resgination, then defiance comes over her.

CLOSE UP

A GENERALS HAT sits tightly on GENERAL WADS head. A stiched badge says "ZOMBIE CONTROL"

CLOSE UP

Chest medals and a name tag saying "GENERAL WAD" are affixed to a white background using blue painters tape.

BACK TO SCENE

The General is also in full Quarantine gear, but with his hat, chest medals belt and boots applied afterwards. It's a marhmallow playing Army. GENERAL WAD comes to a rigid stop, blocking DOLORES path. His hands go behind his back. His faceplate puffs out as he shouts something we can't hear over the music.

DOLORES
What? I can't hear you.

CCTV POV (SILENT)

WAD stomps and points angrily at the speaker. Behind them, a ZOMBIE approaches the window.

DOLORES picks up the speaker and points at it, questioning. WAD is shakes his finger at the headphones and points to his own head.

BACK TO SCENE

DOLORES
(shouting)
I don't think these actually work anymore. What are you doing here so late? I thought I was supposed to be the only one here.

The ZOMBIE is starting to line dance behind them as they argue. He stumbles around, but seems to know his moves. He executes a perfect boot slap, then falls into the window. WAD and DOLORES turn slowly to look at him.

DOLORES raises her phone and hits "STOP".

CLOSE UP

The power on the speaker goes off.

BACK TO SCENE

The ZOMBIE comes it senses, seeming embarrassed. It raises its arms and seems to look for their approval before it starts to wander the room again.

DOLORES
What the fuck was that?

GENERAL WAD
(shouting at the radio before it gets to his mouth)
Doctor Brooks! Max! You have to get down to Sub-basement seventeen RIGHT NOW!!
(to Dolores)
Put on those god-damned headphones!!

He grabs her headphones and slams them on her head. He spins a moment, getting his bearings. Then begins to march past her.

DOLORES lifts her phone and eyes her "Death By Metal" playlist. WAD passes her

DOLORES
(pressing the phone)
Careful. Wet floor.

WAD flails and falls to the floor. He tries to get up and slips again. Once more and he reaches for the cart

CCTV POV (SILENT)

The CCTV is paused on WAD, mouth agape, researches desperately for the cart, DOLORES headphone wire in the middle of the scene.

VOICE ONE (V.O.)
Now, watch.

The camera feed resumes. WAD flails and pulls the headphones off her head, launching them into the air. They fly and the chord is pulled from here phone. The light on the speaker goes bright. DOLORES covers her ears. WAD, writhing on the floor, covers his ears.

The ZOMBIE rushes to the window, holding his ears. DOLORES and WAD both respond to his thrashing. They stare agape as it stares at them in horror. Suddenly, the ZOMBIEs head explodes, sending bile and blood along the entire window.

VOICE TWO (V.O.)
What. The. Fuck.

VOICE ONE (V.O.)
Nobody knows. That's why we called you.

DOLORES and WAD are joined by RESEARCHES and SOLDIERS, all recoiling in shock and horror.
